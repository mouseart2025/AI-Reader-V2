# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in AI Reader V2 to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask for context before escalating.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal data, private novel
  texts, API keys, or logs containing them.
- Follow the project's local-first/privacy principles.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Posting credentials, private datasets, or copyrighted texts you do not have
  the right to share.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer via GitHub
(`@mouseart2025`) or the security contact in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be
reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove
comments, close threads, or block accounts for violations.
